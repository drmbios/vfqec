02 / THE IDEA

A feedback loop with a narrow view.

The optimizer sees syndromes.
The hidden field stays hidden.

01

Measure the disturbance.

Hidden rotations disturb a three-qubit memory. Stabilizer measurements provide a signal without supplying the logical state to the optimizer.

Rₓ(εᵢ) → syndrome statistics
02

Learn a virtual field.

SPSA adjusts per-qubit correction angles to lower the finite-shot syndrome cost. True noise parameters and logical readouts are excluded from its interface.

θ ← measured feedback
03

Compensate each round.

Apply the learned inverse rotations alongside QEC recovery. Compare the observed logical failures against the baselines and oracle.

Rₓ(εᵢ) Rₓ(−θᵢ) ≈ I
